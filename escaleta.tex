\textbf{SINOPSIS} \\

\section{ rip}


\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Phase 1: The Setup \& Casual Partnership}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{The Convenience Store Scam:} Early in the morning, a young con artist named Juan successfully pulls off a bill-changing scam on a cashier. Emboldened, he tries the exact same trick on the next cashier after a shift change, but he is caught.
        \item \textbf{The Fake Arrest:} A man pretending to be a plainclothes police officer intervenes, ``arrests'' Juan, and drags him out of the store. Once outside, the man reveals himself to be Marcos, a veteran con artist. 
        \item \textbf{The Proposition:} Marcos explains his usual partner has mysteriously gone missing and proposes that Juan team up with him for just one day. Juan is highly reluctant but eventually agrees because he desperately needs \$70,000 to bribe a corrupt judge and release his father from prison.
        \item \textbf{The Warm-Up Cons:} Marcos takes Juan around the streets of Buenos Aires to test his skills. They pull off low-level street scams (like tricking a waiter and conning an old lady out of a ring). Juan shows a strong knack for quick improvisation but expresses clear moral discomfort with Marcos’s ruthless exploitation of vulnerable people.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Phase 2: The Bait – The ``Nine Queens''}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{The Emergency Call:} Marcos gets a call from his sister, Valeria, who works at a luxury hotel. She tells him that an old associate of his, an elderly master forger named Sandler, has fallen gravely ill in the hotel lobby.
        \item \textbf{The Forgery Revealed:} Marcos and Juan rush to the hotel. Sandler reveals he has a masterpiece in his possession: a perfect counterfeit sheet of incredibly rare, Weimar-era German stamps known as the ``Nine Queens.'' 
        \item \textbf{The Perfect Mark:} Sandler explains that a wealthy, corrupt Spanish businessman named Vidal Gandolfo is staying at the hotel. Gandolfo is a passionate stamp collector and is being deported the very next morning, meaning he has no time to properly send the stamps to a lab for authentication. He is the ultimate ``patsy.''
        \item \textbf{The Pitch:} Marcos and Juan step into Sandler’s shoes to run the deal. They meet Gandolfo in his hotel suite. Gandolfo brings in a professional philately (stamp) expert to check them out. The expert clears them, and Gandolfo offers a massive \$450,000 for the sheet, scheduling the exchange for that evening.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Phase 3: The Plan Unravels (The Complications)}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{The Blackmail:} Immediately outside the hotel, Gandolfo's stamp expert corners Marcos and Juan. He reveals he \textit{known} the stamps were fakes all along and demands a hefty cut of the profits to stay quiet. 
        \item \textbf{The Motorcycle Thieves:} As Marcos and Juan walk down the street discussing how to pay the expert, two thieves on a motorcycle suddenly swoop in, snatch the bag containing the counterfeit stamps, and speed off. Unaware of what is actually inside the bag, the thieves accidentally dump the stamps into a river, destroying them.
        \item \textbf{Locating the Originals:} Desperate to salvage the deal, Marcos tracks down Berta, the widowed sister of Sandler's deceased former partner. Berta owns the \textit{actual}, genuine Nine Queens stamps. 
        \item \textbf{The Buy-In:} Berta agrees to sell the real stamps, but demands \$250,000 in cash immediately. Marcos says he can put up \$200,000 of his own money, but demands that Juan contribute his life savings—the exact \$50,000 Juan was saving for his father's legal defense. Juan is deeply suspicious but ultimately capitulates, and they buy the real stamps.
        \item \textbf{The Final Hurdle:} When they bring the real stamps back to Gandolfo, he adds a twisted new condition: he will only buy them if Marcos's estranged sister, Valeria, spends the night with him. Marcos begs his sister to do it. Valeria agrees, but only on the condition that Marcos signs over her share of the family inheritance, which Marcos had previously swindled away from her and their younger brother, Federico. Marcos signs the papers.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Phase 4: The Climax \& The Twist Ending}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{The Check:} The next morning, Valeria tells a relieved Marcos and Juan that the deal went through. Gandolfo has taken the real stamps and left a certified manager's check for \$450,000 from a major local bank.
        \item \textbf{The Bank Collapse:} Marcos and Juan rush to the bank to cash the check. Just as they arrive, they find a chaotic, rioting crowd outside. The bank has abruptly failed and gone into bankruptcy due to massive corporate fraud. The \$450,000 check is completely worthless. 
        \item \textbf{The Dispersal:} Juan looks entirely devastated, gives up, and walks away into the bustling city streets. A manic Marcos stays behind, desperately trying to find a loophole to salvage a single cent.
        \item \textbf{The Ultimate Reveal:} The camera follows Juan as he walks into a hidden, empty warehouse. Waiting for him inside are the motorcycle thieves, the convenience store cashiers, the stamp expert, Sandler, Berta, Vidal Gandolfo, Valeria (who is revealed to be Juan's actual girlfriend), and her brother Federico.
        \item \textbf{The Reality:} The entire 24-hour narrative was a massive, elaborately coordinated ``long con'' orchestrated by Juan and Valeria. The counterfeit stamps, the motorcycle theft, the fake Spanish collector, and the sick old man were all theater. Juan's father wasn't even in jail. 
        \item \textbf{The Resolution:} The true goal of the scam was to trick the ruthlessly greedy Marcos into willingly handing over his \textit{real} \$200,000 cash to buy the ``authentic'' stamps from Berta, while simultaneously forcing him to sign over Valeria and Federico's rightful inheritance. The movie ends with the group dividing up Marcos's cash while Juan and Valeria share a triumphant kiss.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
